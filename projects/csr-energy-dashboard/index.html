Top 15 countries by emission intensity (2018)

Tonnes of CO₂e per million USD of GDP. Small economies rank high because the GDP denominator is tiny — always declare this limit. Hover a bar for absolute GHG.

Reading tip: intensity ≠ absolute emissions. Central African Republic leads on intensity but emits far less in absolute terms than Iran or the low-income aggregate.
